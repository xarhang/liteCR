# Community conduct

Treat contributors and testers respectfully. Discuss the work and evidence; do not harass, threaten, discriminate against or publish private information about others. Keep examples synthetic and avoid sharing patient data or credentials.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive or sensitive content, close disruptive discussions and restrict participation where needed. Explain disagreements with concrete, reproducible examples and allow others time to respond.

A confidential conduct-reporting contact has not been designated. Ask a maintainer for a private reporting channel without publishing identifying or sensitive details. For software vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
